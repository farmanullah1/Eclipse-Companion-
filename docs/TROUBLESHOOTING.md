# Troubleshooting Guide

### 1. Earbuds Not Discovered in Scan
- Ensure both earbuds are placed in the charging case and the lid is opened.
- Verify phone Bluetooth is turned on in Android system settings.
- If running Android 12+, ensure "Nearby devices" permission is granted when prompted.
- On older Android versions (Android 11 or below), Location services must be enabled for Bluetooth Low Energy scanning per Android OS security rules.

### 2. Disconnects Mid-Session
- Keep the smartphone within 10 meters (33 feet) line-of-sight of the earbuds.
- Check if battery is critically low (<10%).
- Close any aggressive third-party battery saver applications that may kill background BLE connections.

### 3. Hearing Assist Feedback (High-Pitched Squeal)
- Tap the red "STOP ASSIST NOW" button immediately.
- Lower the Output Level slider to 10%–15%.
- Position the phone microphone at least 1 meter away from your ears pointing toward the speaker.
- Ensure earbuds are seated snugly with properly sized silicone ear tips to prevent acoustic leakage.

### 4. Earbuds Factory Reset Procedure
- Place both earbuds into the charging case.
- With the case lid open, press and hold the multi-function touch pads on both earbuds simultaneously for 8–10 seconds until the LED indicators flash red/white.
- Close the lid for 5 seconds, then open it again to restart pairing mode.
